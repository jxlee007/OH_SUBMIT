The simple rule
When writing measurable outcomes, ask:

“What number would prove that we succeeded?”

A good outcome usually has a metric + target + timeframe.

These aren't random features. They're connected into one business workflow.

Customer → Menu → Order → Payment → Bill → Inventory.

1. Business impact
"Reduce manual order-processing time by 60%."

2. User experience
"A new user can complete the main workflow in under 2 minutes."

3. Technical/system performance
"Dashboard loads in under 1 second for 10,000 records."

"Here is a painful business problem → here is the Odoo workflow → here is our measurable improvement → here is the live demo proving it."
